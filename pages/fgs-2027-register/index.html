Funnel Growth Summit (FGS) is India's biggest funnel, online business and AI marketing event. Across previous editions, 22,000+ entrepreneurs, founders, marketers and creators have attended to learn from some of the biggest names in the industry and build their network.

FGS 2027: AI Marketing Edition is the 7th edition.

When and where is FGS 2027?

FGS 2027: AI Marketing Edition is an in-person event on 17, 18 & 19 February 2027, at Talkatora Stadium, New Delhi.

FGS happens in a stadium, and a stadium has a fixed number of seats. It doesn't matter how many people want to come; once the seats are filled, nobody else can get in.
