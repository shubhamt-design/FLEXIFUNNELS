Questions

Before you add to bag.

Which tie should I pick for thick or curly hair?+

Coil Ties and the Silk Scrunchie. Coils grip with tension spread around the spiral, and the scrunchie's wide fabric holds volume without squashing your curls.

Will they really not leave a dent?+

Scrunchies and coils are the gentlest options, because the pressure is spread over a wider area instead of one tight line. For the least crease, tie it a little looser than you would a standard elastic.

Do coil ties stretch out?+

They loosen with wear — that's normal. Drop a stretched coil into warm water for a minute and it springs back into shape.

How do I wash the scrunchies?+

Hand wash in cool water with a little gentle shampoo, squeeze out the water (don't wring), and let them air-dry flat.

Can I mix different styles and colours?+

Yes — that's the point. Build a set from any five ties in any colours you like.
