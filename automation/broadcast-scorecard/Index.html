How the numbers work

  • Click rate = clicks ÷ delivered. Read rate = reads ÷ delivered. Click-to-read = clicks ÷ reads, which shows how persuasive the message is once opened. Cost / click = AiSensy cost ÷ clicks.
  • vs similar compares a broadcast’s clicks with what similar broadcasts got: same campaign, same timing (1+ days before, webinar morning, 1–6 h before, final hour, during live, next day) and same list size (registered list vs 5,000+). +40% means 40% more clicks than expected.
  • Verdicts are per message (all sends of nearly the same text). Use more: 25%+ above similar with solid evidence. Keep sending: within 10% of similar or better. Improve: below similar, with the likely reason (opened less, or read but not clicked). Remove: 40%+ below similar with solid evidence, or no clicks where some were expected. Not enough data: under 300 delivered or too few expected clicks.
  • Confidence reflects how far the result is from chance, based on the number of clicks expected. High = the gap is very unlikely to be luck.
  • Campaigns are recognised from the message text. Webinar sessions are found from the bursts of live-time sends. Each broadcast is matched to the nearest slot in the Tuesday, Wednesday or Sunday schedule (within 15 minutes around live time, 90 minutes otherwise).
